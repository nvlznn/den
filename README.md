# Den - Focus Timer

Privacy Policy and Support

Last updated: October 2026

Privacy
Den has no advertising or third-party analytics. It uses Apple iCloud for private sync and a public community focus total, and Apple StoreKit for optional character egg purchases.

Your focus records, tags, egg choices and character progress are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they are also stored in your own private iCloud database so they can sync across your devices and be restored after you reinstall the app. This data is accessible only to you through your Apple ID. The developer cannot see it.

Community focus total
Only real timed sessions count. When signed in to iCloud, Den contributes each session’s original duration and an opaque record identifier to a public CloudKit database. These contributions can be read by the developer and other app users. CloudKit also maintains standard metadata such as a pseudonymous creator identifier and upload time. Tags, characters and focus start times are not uploaded to the public database. Manual records are excluded. Editing or deleting a private record does not remove time already contributed. Offline contributions are retried later.

Purchases
Apple processes optional egg purchases. Den verifies repeatable New Egg purchases and saves the delivered characters and purchase receipts in its private iCloud data. Den does not receive payment card details.

Notifications
The app sends one kind of notification only: a local notification when a countdown timer that you started finishes. Notification scheduling takes place on your device.
