# Den - Focus Timer

Privacy Policy and Support

Last updated: October 2026

Privacy
Den does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code.

Your focus records and tags are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they are also stored in your own private iCloud database so they can sync across your devices and be restored after you reinstall the app. This data is accessible only to you through your Apple ID. The developer cannot see it.

Notifications
The app sends one kind of notification only: a local notification when a countdown timer that you started finishes. No data is sent to any server.

Children
The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
