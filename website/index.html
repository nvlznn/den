Focus timer for iPhone

Focus with a tiny friend by your side.

Den is a calm focus timer with a little pixel creature living on a retro LCD screen. Start a session and your friend quietly keeps you company, growing with every hour you spend together.

Free · iPhone · No account needed

Boxy · tap the screen to say hi

Simple on purpose.

Everything you need to sit down and focus, and nothing that nags you when you don't.

Stopwatch or countdown

Count up with no limit, or pick anything from 5 minutes to 3 hours. When time is up you get a gentle nudge, and you can keep going.

Pause when life happens

Pause and resume anytime. Paused time never counts toward your session.

A friend that grows

Every 10 hours of focus is a new level. Six characters to choose from, each with its own level.

Tags and records

Tag sessions like Study or Work. Browse by week, edit a session, or add one you forgot to time.

Statistics

See your focus by day, week, month and year, with a focus calendar, your best hour, averages and a breakdown by tag.

Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

A Live Activity keeps your timer one glance away, even when your phone is locked.

Synced with iCloud

Your records live in your own private iCloud, so they come back when you reinstall or switch phones.

Calm by design

No streaks, no daily goals, and nothing that gets sad while you're away. Your friend is happy whenever you come back.

Meet the den.

Not quite animals, not quite anything. Pick the one you like. Each keeps its own level, so you can raise them all.

A look inside.

Native iOS, Light and Dark Mode, and a little green screen that never changes color.

Your data stays yours.

Den is a quiet little app. It doesn't need to know who you are.

  • No account, no sign-up
  • No ads and no tracking
  • No third-party code or analytics
  • Records sync only through your own private iCloud
